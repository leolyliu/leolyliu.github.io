I'm a PhD candidate in the <a href="https://iiis.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/">Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS)</a> at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. <a href="https://ericyi.github.io/">Li Yi</a>. Currently, I'm also a research intern at <a href="https://www.galbot.com/">GALBOT</a>, focusing on human-to-humanoid skill transfer.

I received my Bachelor's in Computer Science and Technology from Tsinghua University, where I was a research intern at the <a href="https://cg.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/">Graphics and Geometric Computing Lab (CSCG)</a> in <a href="https://www.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/">Department of Computer Science and Technology</a> at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. <a href="https://cg.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/#people.htm#shimin.htm">Shimin Hu</a>. My graduation thesis was supervised by Prof. <a href="https://ericyi.github.io/">Li Yi</a> and Prof. <a href="https://cg.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/people/~mtj/">Taijiang Mu</a>.

<b>Research interests:</b>

* 📦🚶‍♂️Human-Object Interaction: Motion Capture, Motion Generation
* 🤖 Embodied AI: Humanoid Robot Motion Tracking, Vision-based Humanoid Autonomous Systems
